# Audio Transcription

## Definition

Audio transcription is the process of converting spoken words from an audio or
video recording into written text. In AI workflows, this is usually done with
speech-to-text models that read an audio file and return a transcript, often
with optional metadata such as language hints, confidence scores, segments, or
timestamps.

## Context and Usage

Engineering teams use audio transcription to turn meetings, interviews,
product demos, support calls, lectures, and research recordings into text that
can be searched, reviewed, summarized, translated, or passed into downstream AI
systems. A reproducible transcription workflow usually includes a stable
runtime, a clear provider configuration, a small validation recording, and a
review step before the transcript is used as source material.
